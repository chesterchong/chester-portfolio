# Your reality is fake because it's your perception

The reality about this world is your perceptions. It will be proven skewed and fake, if you live long enough and lucky to see the truth behind everything.

I read a book before, but I forgot it's name. It is something about the reality that you believed in is not a reality. 

A group of people were locked in a room with a small window since they were born. They've never seen anything like colors before, only shadows appearing outside the window. When they grew up, you'll never be able to convince them that there are colors in this world.

Something like that, idk hahaha.

There were lots of people who been saying this but doing that. For example, a girl who been saying she incapable of loving someone, but ended up with a sexual partner the next day.

The wealthy dudes who been saying "I don't care about money" are the ones who has been earning millions if not billions of dollars. The capitalist who bought into real estates, then rented out to the poors, and make more money to get richer.

In the world of love, people said I love you everyday but ended up cheating on his/her partner. Is this truly love? and, people who enjoy one night stand can still find a partner regardless of their past experience.

I don't believe that there is a karma, but I believe there is luck component in everything. When you are unlucky, something bad happens to you, and people misinterpreted it as a karma for something you've done in the past, while in fact you did nothing.

Good people die early and this is true. Chester Bennington, Avicii, and Michael Jackson for examples. They have been murdered if not committed suicide because of the bad people did to them.

A girl can say "I enjoy being single" or "I'd rather focusing on career/academics" and still ended up with a partner the very next day. The worst case is a "no" but they won't say anything to you. The stereotypes are earned, not given.

There is something we called "反差" in chinese, whereby a person looks like naive, pure, innocent, and kind, but she/he is actually someone who is opposite in life - cynical, malicious, horny, guilty, depraved, and more.

Sometimes, the people that we seen as "bad" are not entirely bad. They might be doing for good deeds than the ones who are seen as the "good" ones.

There has been a saying that, your closest friends and family are the ones who wish you don't do well in your life. Be as broke as you can, be unsuccessful as they'd hope. 

> “We want our friends to succeed, but never more than us.”

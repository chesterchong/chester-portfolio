# Just venting

Something came up to my mind - why do people always think at the surface area?

I've seen so many people, most of friends, family, and colleagues. They are most likely think at the surface area.

For example, when they were given an order, they will execute without reasoning further. Let's demonstrate this in 2 scenarios:

Scenario A - Client Meeting:

Let's just say our manager wanted to get a coffee for his client, so he asked person A and person B to buy a coffee. 

Person A bought the coffee for the manager and his client, only then he found out that the client only drink iced coffee without sugar.

Person B however asked for their preferences before buying coffee for the manager and his client. Ended up signing on a business deal for his boss.

Scenario B - Procurement task:

We have a coffee shop, and the boss wanted to make a new coffee product so he had to procure new materials from new oversea suppliers. The boss asked person A and B to do the same task without telling them the plans to make a new coffee product. He simply told them to survey the price of Arabian Coffee Beans.

Person A did his job, surveyed the price and presented to the boss. Yes, the task is completed but it did not help the boss to make decision better, and it requires explicit and detailed commands from the boss to execute something.

But person B did it differently, he took the ownership of the problem, thought what is the reason to do that and what we expected to get out of this execution.
He then surveyed the market of the coffee bean, listed down pros and cons, including the procurement cost, logistic cost, time cost, wholesaler price, small order price, the plans for the long term and short term. All these things to help the boss make better decision.

TL:DR, we should stop answering questions, and start answering decisions. It's not like if you are homeless, just get a house problem.

95% of people including all my friends and family never think at a deeper level. It is called 底层逻辑 or first principle I would say. I am bad at expressing my thoughts so yeah I used AI claude to refine my logics, the sequence of the story, and the overall writings.
